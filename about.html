About

More than two decades of immigration law in New York.

Daniyar Nurtayev has practiced immigration law in New York for 25 years. He founded Nurtayev Law so that every client works directly with an experienced attorney, from the first consultation to the final decision.

His practice covers the full immigration journey: family petitions and green cards, citizenship, work visas, deportation defense, asylum, DACA and TPS, and waivers.

Daniyar advises clients in English, Russian, and Kazakh, so nothing important gets lost in translation.

He is a graduate of Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law and is admitted to practice law in New York. Because immigration law is federal, he represents clients across the United States.
