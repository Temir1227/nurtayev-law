Daniyar Nurtayev

Founder & Attorney | Nurtayev Law P.C.

I am a New York-admitted attorney with more than 25 years of legal experience spanning Kazakhstan and the United States.

Having personally experienced the immigration process, I understand that immigration cases are about much more than paperwork. They affect people's families, careers, security, and futures.

Today, I represent individuals, families, and professionals in immigration matters, including employment-based petitions, humanitarian protection, and proceedings before U.S. Immigration Courts.

My practice is built on a simple principle: every client deserves direct communication with their attorney, honest legal advice, and careful attention to their individual circumstances.

Based in Manhattan, I represent clients throughout the United States in federal immigration matters.

In My Own Words

From Immigrant to Immigration Attorney

My legal career began in Kazakhstan more than 25 years ago. Over the years, I gained experience in legal practice, corporate management, and business, working with organizations across different industries. That background taught me that effective legal representation requires not only knowledge of the law, but also sound judgment, practical experience, and an understanding of the people behind every case.

When I immigrated to the United States, I experienced firsthand the challenges of starting a new life in a different country. I understand the uncertainty of navigating immigration procedures, waiting for important decisions, and building a future for yourself and your family.

Determined to continue my legal career, I earned a Master of Laws (LL.M.) degree from the Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law and was admitted to the New York Bar in 2026.

My professional experience in the United States extends beyond immigration law. I have worked on matters involving civil litigation, employment disputes, personal injury, real estate, and estates and trusts. This broader experience has strengthened my approach to legal analysis, case preparation, and courtroom advocacy.

I founded Nurtayev Law P.C. because I believe that the relationship between an attorney and a client should be built on trust, accessibility, and personal responsibility.

Too often, people seeking legal assistance struggle to communicate directly with the attorney responsible for their case. I believe clients deserve better. They should understand their legal options, know what is happening with their case, and have meaningful access to their attorney.

My approach is straightforward: I listen carefully, explain the law in understandable terms, and remain personally involved in the legal matters entrusted to my firm.

I communicate with clients in English and Russian. Although my office is located in Manhattan, I represent clients throughout the United States in federal immigration matters.

For me, practicing immigration law is not simply about preparing applications or appearing in court. It is about helping people navigate some of the most important decisions of their lives with dignity, clarity, and confidence.
